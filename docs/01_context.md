\# Context \& Vision – CRM Lite (Agencias de Viajes)



\## Product Vision

Build a lightweight, internal CRM to manage travel agencies as leads and clients,

focused on clarity, traceability, and operational speed.



This CRM is not a public SaaS. It is an internal tool for a small team.



\## Target Users

\- 3 internal users

\- All users have superadmin privileges

\- No public registration

\- No external access



\## Core Problem

Currently, agency information is fragmented (WhatsApp, Excel, memory).

There is no unified view of:

\- Who was contacted

\- What was said

\- Who changed what

\- What is duplicated



\## Product Goals

\- Centralize agency and branch data

\- Track commercial status clearly

\- Keep a full audit trail of all changes

\- Enable bulk onboarding via CSV/Excel

\- Be usable on mobile and desktop



\## Non-Goals

\- No marketing automation

\- No WhatsApp API

\- No email campaigns

\- No multi-company support

\- No advanced role system



This product prioritizes clarity over complexity.
